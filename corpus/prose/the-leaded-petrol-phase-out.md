# The leaded petrol phase-out, and how the dates were assembled

## Quoted from Our World in Data's own dataset description

> This dataset reports the year for which leaded petrol (Tetraethyllead) was banned for use in road vehicles. Algeria was the final country to ban leaded petrol in road vehicles in 2021. The specific date of phase-out could not be found for some countries (although all countries have banned the use of leaded petrol in road vehicles).
>
> This dataset reports the year for which leaded petrol (Tetraethyllead) was banned for use in road vehicles. Algeria was the final country to ban leaded petrol in road vehicles in 2021. The specific date of phase-out could not be found for some countries (although all countries have banned the use of leaded petrol in road vehicles). This dataset was built by Our World in Data based on published dates from multiple sources. These sources are listed here: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tetraethyllead https://unece.org/DAM/trans/doc/2017/itc/UNEP_14_used_vehicles_Geneva-EMD.pdf https://ec.europa.eu/jrc/sites/default/files/jrc105673.pdf https://lead.org.au/Chronology-Making_Leaded_Petrol_History.pdf https://www.un.org/esa/gite/cleanfuels/ifqc-globaloverview.pdf https://www.reglugerd.is/reglugerdir/eftir-raduneytum/umhverfisraduneyti/nr/4507 https://timesofmalta.com/articles/view/leaded-petrol-replacement-to-go-on-sale-from-thursday.159938
